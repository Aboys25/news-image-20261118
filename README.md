# 18 November 2026 — The World in One Day

Entry for make.fun competition **news-image-20261118**: one image summing up
the world's news of Wednesday, 18 November 2026. A newspaper front page with
a large "18 NOVEMBER 2026" masthead and six illustrated panels, each with a
short caption. The program (`main.py`, Python 3.13, stdlib only) decodes the base64-encoded
image (`entry.png.b64`) and writes it to the output directory as
`entry.png`; it uses no network. The image is stored as base64 so the
repository contains text files only.

## The six stories (all happened or first reported on 18 November 2026)

1. **Iran strikes Kuwait and Bahrain** — Early on 18 Nov, Iran launched
   strikes against Kuwait's Ali Al Salem Air Base (which hosts U.S. forces)
   and Bahrain, after the U.S. struck a missile site on Qeshm Island.
   Sources: Just Security Early Edition, 18 Nov 2026
   (https://www.justsecurity.org/165038/early-edition-november-18-2026/),
   citing the Wall Street Journal and Reuters.
2. **Crimea bridge falls** — After Ukrainian drone and missile strikes on
   16–17 Nov, the Kerch Bridge's central road arch collapsed into the strait;
   the collapse was widely reported on 18 Nov, with road traffic suspended
   and two people killed. Sources: Reuters via BusinessWorld
   (https://bworldonline.com/world/2026/11/18/794660/ukraine-cripples-crimean-road-bridge-in-blow-to-putin-before-meeting-with-xi-and-trump/),
   Bloomberg (https://www.europesays.com/ukraine/73327/).
3. **Xi meets Putin in Shenzhen** — Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin met in
   Shenzhen on 18 Nov, a day before their planned trilateral summit with
   Donald Trump; Xi pledged to stand against trade protectionism.
   Sources: Just Security Early Edition, 18 Nov 2026; Open Chronicle
   (https://openchronicle.org/2026/11/18/global-news-brief-key-developments-around-the-world-november-18-2026/),
   citing AFP and AP.
4. **Peltola wins Alaska Senate** — Democrat Mary Peltola defeated Sen. Dan
   Sullivan (R); the last Senate race to be called, giving Democrats a 56–44
   majority. Source: Associated Press, reported 18 Nov 2026, via Just
   Security Early Edition
   (https://www.justsecurity.org/165038/early-edition-november-18-2026/).
5. **Ebola passes 11,000 cases** — The DR Congo Ebola outbreak more than
   doubled in three months to over 11,000 cases and about 5,400–5,500 deaths;
   the UN appealed for support on 18 Nov. Sources: UN News, 18 Nov 2026
   (https://europeansting.com/2026/11/18/world-news-in-brief-ebola-cases-double-in-dr-congo-chemical-exposure-starts-before-birth-turk-concern-over-eu-rwanda-transfer-plan/);
   Reuters, 17–18 Nov 2026.
6. **Cyclone Xavier hits Cook Islands** — Severe Tropical Cyclone Xavier
   reached Category 5 over the Northern Cook Islands on 18 Nov, with gusts
   up to 315 km/h; wharf damage on Manihiki, no casualties reported.
   Sources: RNZ Pacific / MetService, 18 Nov 2026
   (https://www.metservice.com/warnings/tropical-cyclone-activity);
   One Turn, 18 Nov 2026 (https://oneturn.news/news/).

## Files
